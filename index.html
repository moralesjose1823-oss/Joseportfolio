WHO I AM

I fell in love with sports long before I knew I wanted to work in the industry.

At 14, I started reselling shoes. I loved the game, the shoes, and everything that came with it. More than anything, I loved learning how to hustle — figuring out how to turn one dollar into two, finding opportunities, and putting in the work to make something happen.

That experience taught me how to connect with people, understand what they value, and build something from the ground up. It’s the same mindset I bring to athlete partnerships and sports business today.

I'm studying Advertising with a Sports Business minor at the University of Oregon. Between retail operations, Minor League Baseball, and NIL partnerships, I've learned that the part of sports I enjoy most is helping people turn an opportunity into something real.

BASEBALL & PURPOSE

Baseball has always been more than a game to me. It's where I learned to compete, keep showing up, and believe in something bigger than myself.

Growing up, I watched my mom work three jobs to support us. I learned early what it takes just to get by, and I carried that work ethic into everything I did, especially baseball.

Then, as a young teenager, I broke my ankle. I spent three months in bed and eight months recovering in total. Going from playing the game I loved to having to learn how to walk again changed the way I looked at my future. I had a lot of time to think about what I wanted to do with my life and what kind of impact I could make.

That experience helped me realize that my place in baseball didn't have to end when I stepped off the field. I could still be part of the game by helping other athletes navigate their own journeys, especially when things don't go as planned.

I want to be the person an athlete can turn to — a resource, a supporter, and someone who understands that there's a whole person behind the performance.

That's why I'm excited to build my career in the baseball ecosystem. Whether it's athlete representation, partnerships, player development, or the business side of the sport, I want to help players find opportunities, make informed decisions, and get where they want to go. I know what it feels like to have the game taken away for a while. I want to help athletes feel supported through the highs, the setbacks, and everything in between.
